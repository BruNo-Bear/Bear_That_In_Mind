01About

Hi, I’m Bruce.

I’m an IT support specialist and Cisco-trained network technician based in Katlehong. Over the past several years I’ve repaired hardware, secured networks, and taught digital skills to hundreds of young people and adults through community and government-backed programmes across the country.

I like solving real problems: a laptop that won’t boot, a network that keeps dropping, a classroom that has never touched a circuit board. On a help desk, at a workbench or in front of a room of new learners, I bring the same attention to detail and follow-through. Outside formal roles I run Bear Tech Solutions, my own IT support and training practice.
